01

The flowers are the colour

A palette in full bloom

Each brand colour comes with five tints, mixed into ivory. Tap a chip to copy its hex. Sage and blush are the two primaries, and Evening Fern is the ink: it’s used for every line, every shadow and all text. There’s no black anywhere.

🌼
Butter is the joy colour, used only as a fill. It works as a fill, a wash or a sticker behind fern line, and never as text or a line on ivory, where it disappears.
